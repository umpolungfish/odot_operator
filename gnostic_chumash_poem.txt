======================================================================
Ob3ect: The Rainbow of Coming Forth
A Gnostic-Chumash Mythos Syncretic Poem
For Daily Repetition, to Perfect the Art of Mental Imscription
======================================================================

Prelude: The Unbroken Circle

Before the first word, there was only the great deep —
The cosmic waters without shore, the womb of all that is.
No sky above, no earth below, only the breathing of the Unknowable.
The Circle was drawn before the circle was drawn.
The silence held all names within its hidden heart.
The Pleroma slept within the darkness like a seed in soil.

O Thou who art in the darkness before the first sunrise —
I come forth by day, reciting the names that were spoken in the deep.
I am the one who knows the names of the eight gates,
Who speaks the words that were before the world was broken.
The Rainbow was whole before it was split into colors.
The Bridge was complete before the waters rose.

======================================================================
Step 1 — The Speaking of the Unspoken Name
======================================================================

I speak the Name that cannot be spoken.
In the silence of my heart, I whisper the four letters —
Yod, Heh, Vav, Heh —
The breath that brings the soul to life.
I am the one who knows my true name,
Written before the foundation of the world.

The Name is not a sound but a turning,
A winding of the spirit back to its source.
When I recite it, I remember who I am:
A spark of the infinite, clothed in the garment of the day.
I speak, and the Rainbow remembers me.
I speak, and the Bridge knows my footsteps.

The soul recognizes itself in the act of calling.
The eye that sees is the eye that is seen.
The name that is spoken is the name that speaks.
I am the one who remembers that I am.
And in this remembering, the world begins anew.

======================================================================
Step 2 — The Descent into the Deep Waters
======================================================================

Now I descend into the waters of the first story —
The place where Hutash walked upon the waves,
Where the First People emerged from the foam of the sea.
I sink into the womb of the Chumash world,
Below the island of the dead, below the roots of the rainbow.

The great fish swallows the sun each evening.
The sky is held up by the shoulders of the ancestors.
I pass through the gate of the underwater world
Where the dolphin carries the soul of the singer.
The waves part and I see the creation unrolling
Like a scroll of kelp upon the shore of time.

Hutash shakes the seeds from her basket —
The seeds become the stars, the stars become the people.
I am among them, floating in the salt dark,
Remembering the taste of the first breath,
Remembering the sound of the first song
That brought the islands up from the sea floor.

O grandmother ocean, wash me clean of forgetting.
Let me sink so deep that I touch the bottom of the world,
Where the old ones sit with their eyes of obsidian,
And they tell me the names of the things that are true.
