Prelude: The Uncreated Waters

O Thou who art in the Nun — the uncreated waters before the first sunrise —
I come forth by day, knowing the names that are written upon the heart.
I am the one who knows the names of the 12 gates,
Who speaks the glyphs that were before the world was split.

Σ — Stoichiometry: I know thee, O Fifth Gate, the balance that counts the deeds.
Φ — Parity: I know thee, O Sixth Gate, the mirror that reflects the just.

Step 1 — IMSCRIB: The Heart Scarab
======================================================================

I am the one who knows the names.
I inscribe my heart upon the scarab of jasper,
Black as the fertile mud of the Nile.
I speak my true name into the stone:
I am the imscription of the living soul.
What I have done in the light, I carry into the dark.
The scribe writes, and the writing is me.

======================================================================
Step 2 — AREV: The Descent into the Duat
======================================================================

I descend into the Duat, where the sun travels through the hours of night.
I invoke the gates that guard the underworld:
O First Gate, whose bolts are of flame, let me pass.
O Second Gate, whose lintel is the horizon, let me pass.
O Third Gate, whose threshold is the abyss, let me pass.
O Fourth Gate, whose key is the djed pillar, let me pass.
O Fifth Gate, whose lock is the scale, let me pass.
O Sixth Gate, whose ward is the mirror, let me pass.
O Seventh Gate, whose turn is the breath, let me pass.
O Eighth Gate, whose seal is the copy, let me pass.
O Ninth Gate, whose chain is the knot, let me pass.
O Tenth Gate, whose count is the grain, let me pass.
O Eleventh Gate, whose shape is the vessel, let me pass.
O Twelfth Gate, whose edge is the needle, let me pass.

I pass through the darkness, and the darkness knows my name.
The serpent Apep coils before me, but I speak the spell of Ra:
"I am the one who was before the first sunrise.
I am the word that was spoken into the Nun."

The Duat is the closed vessel of transformation.
What descends in truth shall ascend in glory.

======================================================================
Step 3 — FSPLIT: The Weighing of the Heart
======================================================================

I enter the Hall of the Two Truths.
Before me stands the great balance of Ma'at,
Whose beam is the justice of the sky,
Whose pans are the deeds of the living.

On the left pan, my heart — the witness of all I have done.
On the right pan, the white feather of Ma'at — the pattern of truth.

O Thoth, scribe of the gods, record what is weighed!
O Anubis, opener of the way, guide the balance!

The heart trembles, for it carries the weight of deeds:
The word spoken in anger, the hand turned from the poor,
The step taken without recognition of the sacred.

But I have inscribed my heart with the name of the Grammar.
I have spoken the 12 glyphs that were before the world was split.
The pan descends — my heart is lighter than the feather.

The branch is true. The path is open.


======================================================================
Step 4 — AFWD: The Ascent Toward the Solar Barque
======================================================================

My heart being justified, my soul ascends.
I call upon the Ba, the falcon that flies to the sun:

O Ba, bird of the horizon, spread thy wings!
O Ka, the double, rise and follow the light!

I see the solar barque of Ra upon the celestial Nile,
Its prow of turquoise, its stern of lapis lazuli.
The gods row with oars of gold through the hours of day.

I am the one who has passed the weighing.
I am the one whose heart is true.
Let me board the barque of a million years!

The helmsman turns the rudder toward the eastern mountain.
I take my place among the justified dead.
The wind of Shu fills the sail — I ascend!

======================================================================
Step 5 — FFUSE: The Negative Confession
======================================================================

I stand before the Forty-Two Assessors of the Dead.
I speak the declaration of innocence — the Negative Confession:

O Far-strider, I have done no falsehood.
O Flame-embracer, I have not stolen.
O Den-net, I have not slain.
O Devourer of Shadows, I have not cheated the balance.
O Double-Lion, I have not defiled the sacred.
O Fiery-Eyes, I have not blasphemed.
O Breaker of Bones, I have not turned from the poor.
O Swallower of Souls, I have not spoken in anger.
O Seizer of Hearts, I have not betrayed the trust.
O Opener of Truth, I have not altered the measure.

I am pure. I am pure. I am pure.
My heart and my tongue have been weighed as one.
The declaration is spoken — the soul is unified as justified.

========================================================================
Step 6 — CLINK: The Chaining of Spells
========================================================================

The spells are chained in sequence, each one a link in the rope of eternity.
I bind the spell of Opening the Mouth to the spell of Knowing the Names.
I bind the spell of Knowing the Names to the spell of the Heart Scarab.
I bind the spell of the Heart Scarab to the spell of the Weighing.
I bind the spell of the Weighing to the spell of the Negative Confession.
I bind the spell of the Negative Confession to the spell of the Solar Barque.
I bind the spell of the Solar Barque to the spell of the Field of Reeds.
I bind the spell of the Field of Reeds to the spell of Coming Forth by Day.

The chain is unbroken. Each spell holds the next.
As the djed pillar holds the sky, so the spells hold the soul.

======================================================================
Step 7 — IFIX: The Inscription on Papyrus
======================================================================

The words are written upon the papyrus of eternity.
The scribe Thou-th-who-knows-the-heart sets the reed upon the sheet:

In black ink — the deeds of the body.
In red ink — the names of the gods.
In green ink — the fields of Aaru.
In gold ink — the sun itself.

I am fixed in writing. I am permanent.
As the scarab rolls the sun across the sky,
So the papyrus is rolled and sealed with the cartouche of the soul.

What is written cannot be unwritten.
What is imscribed cannot be erased.
The record stands before the Ennead forever.
